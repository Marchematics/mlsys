# R147 — Independent claim audit: findings and dispositions

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Method:** two read-only audits of `PAMI_MUR/paper/sections/6_experiments.tex`
(963 lines) and `TKDE_MUR/paper/sections/7_experiments.tex` (610 lines), covering
every quantitative comparative claim, with paired target-level bootstrap
recomputation from `results/raw/**`. Audits wrote nothing; fixes were applied by
hand and are listed below.

## Audit outcome

Roughly 44 claim clusters were verified as resolved and reproducible; the
quantitative core holds up (regime-B table and paired intervals, budget
`k*=3`, pool-size scaling, adaptation sweep, adaptive rule, novelty, the
second family, compute). **15–21 claims were flagged**, and they fall into two
classes.

## Class 2 — numbers with no artifact: re-checked in round 12

The auditors were scoped to `PAMI_MUR/results/`, but most of the evidence lives
in the sibling `KBS_MUR/results/` tree. Re-searching both trees resolved almost
every item.

1. ~~**`tab:headroom-real`** and the "960 / five seeds" framing.~~ **REBUILT.**
   No five-seed real-data run exists (every six-dataset run uses seeds
   101/202/303), so the table was recomputed from the 576 available cells and
   the framing corrected to three seeds. New artifact:
   `results/derived/R134_six_dataset_matrix/headroom.json`. The substantive
   claim is unchanged and now exact: **576 of 576 cells positive**, minimum
   `.0101`, means `.076`–`.119`. (The old values were `.001`–`.003` higher,
   consistent with a longer run that no longer exists.)
2. ~~**`tab:redundancy`**~~ **RESOLVED, exact on all twelve cells.**
   `KBS_MUR/results/raw/R070_oracle_ladder_k16_b4_rgrid_s5_20260919_1540/aggregate.json`
   reproduces standalone `.6880/.5161/.3512/.1771`, learned
   `.6636/.5027/.3652/.2085`, sequential `.6880/.5813/.4471/.2481` and headroom

   written.
3. ~~**Measurement 2 aggregates.**~~ **REPLACED.** The unbacked six-cell,
   1{,}500-train paragraph was deleted from PAMI in favour of the
   artifact-backed single-cell version that matches `R107_observability`

## Class 3 — editorial defects (fixed or listed)

* Two duplicated paragraphs in `6_experiments.tex` **removed**; the
  unbacked six-cell probe paragraph was deleted in favour of the artifact-backed
  single-cell version, which matches `R107_observability` exactly.
* Literal Markdown emphasis: **22 occurrences converted** to `\emph{}` across
  `6_experiments.tex`, `7_discussion.tex` and the TKDE experiment section.
* Ladder table stale cells **fixed** (ridge sequential `.1633` → `.1687`;
  MLP sequential `.1123` → `.1198`), the "flat" sequential range corrected to
  `.096`–`.120`, and the learned-gain factor corrected from `3.6` to `3.0`.
* Expert-row count for full reranking corrected from `68` to `64`
  (`R108_compute_metrla` reports `response_full` = 64 calls).
* The probe's "per-cell spread of `.09`" replaced with the computed standard
  deviation `.064` over the six cells.

## Net effect

The audit did not overturn the paper's thesis; it removed a systematic bias
toward the authors' method. Every correction moved a claim from "wins" toward
"wins where the paired intervals say so": the six-dataset headline is now three
significant wins, two unresolved and one loss, and the masked-mean backbone
result is now a tie at `q=8` rather than a win. The remaining open items are
concentrated in tables that predate this session's artifact discipline.
